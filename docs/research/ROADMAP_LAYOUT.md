# A road through the detailed workflow

Roadmap is the default organization of Connected branches. It preserves the same twelve main boxes, fifteen directed dependencies, and 303 detailed parts. Main boxes occupy fixed positions down the page; opening a branch reveals its recorded parts in a side bay at that stage. The geometry is for reading dependencies, not for estimating financial influence.

## Follow the route

The central route goes from Market observations through Availability and clock, Forecast question, Features and universe, and Return and risk model. It then forks into New-stock review and Holding review. Both paths join Cash and risk checks, followed by Daily paper review, Paper state ledger, and Later outcomes.

Holdings and cash is a separate supplied input. It feeds Holding review and Cash and risk checks; it is not a step produced by the model. The other source-declared shortcuts—Forecast question to Model and Daily paper review to Later outcomes—remain visible. The drawing does not establish that a daily feed, predictor or execution pipeline is running.

## Read without losing your place

- Choose a stop on the road guide to open and focus that stage. Choosing an already-open stage does not close it.
- Click a main box to unfold or collapse its section. Several sections can remain open.
- Select specific parts and reveal their recorded formulas, definitions and component names. These annotations stay separate from the part count.
- Pan and zoom to explore. Drag detailed boxes if useful; Restore roadmap returns the open sections to their organized positions.
- Use the crosslink control to inspect quieter cross-stage detail dependencies. The main workflow and its fifteen dependencies remain available.
- Use the explicit overview control to see the wider network. The starting view favors readable boxes rather than shrinking the entire long road into one screen.

On narrow screens the road guide becomes a location picker with Previous and Next controls. These browse locations; they do not execute stages or turn the separate portfolio input into a sequential processing step. The reading view and record picker remain available.

## Preserved evidence and limits

The five reviewed public JSON documents are unchanged. The catalog still contains 76 categories and 227 leaves, 454 curated detailed assertions, 59 legacy variable definitions, 67 exact formula fields and 30 unique code-component names. Thirty-six additional display-reference pairs are derived only from exact declared input/output IDs; see RADIAL_EXPANSION.md for their provenance rules. Structural and annotation links have separate counts.

The readiness view adds separately reviewed operational colors and completion conditions; see WORKFLOW_READINESS.md. Colors and filters do not change the underlying research results or verify new operating connections.

This is a viewer organization change. It does not rerun models, collect market data, update Neo4j or Notion, change research readiness, or authorize trading. No model is promoted.

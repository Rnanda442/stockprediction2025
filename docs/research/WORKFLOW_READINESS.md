# Readiness on the project roadmap

The roadmap separates operational readiness from research results. A documented dependency is not proof that software or data actually flows through it. A completed experiment is not an approved predictor.

## Color and words

- **Connected — green:** a current connection has evidence verifying its stated purpose and scope.
- **Needs connection — amber:** a component or design exists, but a connection, configuration, decision or observation is still required.
- **Blocked — red:** a required prerequisite is missing or has failed its stated gate.
- **Historical — gray:** previous research, a preserved definition, a paused branch or a superseded approach. Historical does not mean useless.

Every color has a written status. Research-result badges are separate and retain the study's target, baseline and limitations. Passing a software check does not establish predictive skill. No model is promoted.

The current records do not verify a continuous end-to-end daily paper service. In particular, built cash-account mechanics, a proposal log, ticket formatting and metadata guards are separate capabilities. Neither a successful collection transport nor a configured schedule establishes a complete input date or a genuine advance prediction lock. Green is never inferred just because a record says completed or built.

## Use the view

Current workflow emphasizes the present design and its dependencies. Needs attention emphasizes remaining decisions and blockers. Past research exposes preserved historical work. Everything retains access to the full catalog. Context and group records may remain visible so filtered parts are understandable; context is not a matching result or a new scientific part.

Selecting a stage or part explains its purpose, required inputs, existing work, research evidence, missing connection and completion evidence. Main connection details distinguish a documented dependency from operational verification. The roadmap, part picker and reading alternative remain available.

## Sources, freshness and omissions

`workflow-readiness.public.json` is a separate reviewed documentary supplement, bound to the frozen public projections. It preserves the five base JSON documents. Its status mapping retains the original recorded statuses and source IDs rather than replacing the evidence. The maintainer builder is `scripts/build_workflow_readiness_catalog.py`. It refuses changed source hashes; a changed base requires a reviewed mapping and new publication rather than silently retaining old colors.

The displayed review date is the date of the documentary evidence, not a claim that a collector, model or paper service ran that day. This website is a published snapshot. Neo4j, Notion and the website have separate publication acknowledgements and no automatic synchronization.

Personal holdings, quantities, settled cash, account configuration and credentials are not included. Raw price databases, complete notebooks, forecast payloads and private artifact paths remain outside this public-safe viewer. A generic requirement for supplied portfolio state can be shown without exposing the portfolio itself. Reviewed repository sources and the research gate remain authoritative.

Changing a color, expanding a box or applying a filter never runs a model, collects data, places an order or authorizes an experiment. These controls organize reviewed knowledge.
